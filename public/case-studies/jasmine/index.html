She was working harder and earning less for it.

How a LinkedIn brand strategist replaced guesswork pricing with a system, and grew her monthly income by 40 percent.

Client Jasmine Gathoga
Role LinkedIn Brand Strategist & Designer

01 — The Situation

A business that looked successful from the outside, and felt unsustainable from the inside.

Jasmine had real clients, real demand, and an audience that trusted her. What she did not have was a reason for any of her prices. They had settled over time, rather than being decided.

She could tell you what she charged. She could not tell you why.

She was managing a small team to deliver the visible parts of her business, while running the invisible parts almost entirely by herself, the work that built her credibility and kept her pipeline warm. The split was backwards. The work taking most of her week was consistently the work producing the least of her income.

She could not say, with any confidence, what a single client actually cost her to serve. That meant she could not say, with any confidence, whether a client was worth keeping at the rate she had agreed to.

02 — What Was Built

Six weeks. Four systems. Nothing left to guesswork.

The engagement ran as a structured Revenue Operating System, ten sessions across four phases: diagnosis, design, implementation, and handoff. Four systems came out of it.

i.

A Cost of Delivery Framework

Every service she offered was repriced against what it actually costs to deliver, not what felt reasonable at the time. Pricing stopped being a feeling and became a calculation she could defend to anyone.

ii.

A Rebuilt Offer Architecture

Her flagship offer went from a list of features with a price attached, to a structure where every component carried its own market value before the offer price was ever named.

iii.

A Client Acquisition System

A qualification step, a rule for reserving her time, and a rhythm for asking for referrals, solving the quiet constraint that was costing her clients before pricing ever entered the conversation.

iv.

A Financial Operating System

A live, monthly picture of where her money goes and what she actually keeps, replacing a business run on instinct with one she can see clearly, every month.

03 — What Changed

Not a feeling. A number she can point to.

+40%

Once the system made clear exactly what her business was producing, we were able to justify a pricing increase that grew her monthly income by 40 percent.

Pricing Onboarded a new client at a rate meaningfully above anything she had charged before, and defended every line of it without hesitation.
Delegation Fully handed off one of the services that used to consume the most hours on her calendar. It now runs on a real margin, without her touching the execution.
Focus Narrowed her attention to the two offers producing most of her revenue, and let the rest either get systemised or fall away.
Momentum Closed a second client at a higher rate in the weeks that followed, using the same framework, without needing to build anything new for the conversation.
