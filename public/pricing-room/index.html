The problem

Your pricing problem may not be the number.

You can be good at what you do and still struggle with pricing. Maybe you are not sure how you arrived at your current price. Maybe you feel uncomfortable saying the number. Maybe clients push back and you immediately start reconsidering it. Or maybe you simply do not have a consistent way to decide what to charge.

  • You know you are probably undercharging.
  • You look at what other people charge before deciding.
  • You calculate your time and build your price around it.
  • You know your price but struggle when a client pushes back.
  • You reduce your price too quickly when someone says it is expensive.
  • You are confident about your work but not always confident about your price.
